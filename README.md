# Amir Mohammadikarbalaei

Data Scientist and AI Engineer working on NLP, LLMs and the data engineering around them. Currently at Unilever, building automation over service desk and knowledge data, alongside an MSc in Data Science at the University of Bath.

Most of what I enjoy sits between a model and production: the extraction, the evaluation harness, the thing that has to run unattended at 3am.

My work is pinned below, with fuller write-ups at [amir-data.vercel.app](https://amir-data.vercel.app/).
